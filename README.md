## Naturally Spawning Camels
There are five Bactrian Camel variants that spawn in the Badlands biomes: White Mocha, Chamomile, Pumpkin Spice, Turkish Coffee, and Hojicha. When bred with cactus, these variants will blend their colors together. Bactrian Camels can also breed with the Vanilla Camel to pass on colors

## Camel Inventory
The Bactrian Camel can only have one rider, but as a trade off, it can equip a chest for extra storage. You can also equip a dye to change the texture of the chests on the camel
